# Mastery Brief — annotated template

Copy this structure for the deliverable. The bracketed prompts tell you what each part is *for*; delete them in the final output. Keep prose tight and facilitation-friendly.

---

# Mastery Brief: [Topic]
*Prepared for teaching to: [audience] · Why it matters now: [one line]*

## 1. Executive overview
**The hook / why this matters:** [1–2 sentences a beginner feels in their gut — the stakes, the change, the pain.]
**Simple definition:** [One sentence, no jargon. If a term is unavoidable, define it inline.]
**Why now:** [What changed in the world/market/tech/regulation that makes this urgent.]
**The problem it solves:** [What goes wrong without it.]
**Common misunderstanding:** [The thing newcomers assume that is wrong — and the correction.]
**Why it matters to *this* audience:** [Tie directly to their roles and decisions.]
